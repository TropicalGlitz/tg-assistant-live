## Basecoats

Basecoats
Tropical Glitz Basecoats are crafted with the highest quality ingredients to deliver a stunning finish on
your project. This is a solid basecoat with no transparency. For optimal results, we suggest spraying it
over a grey ground coat. Additionally, we highly recommend mixing this basecoat at a 1:1 ratio with one
of our Tropical Glitz Premium Reducers to achieve the best outcome. Remember to select the Tropical
Glitz Reducer that is suitable for the temperature where you are painting. Our Basecoats are available in
RTS (Ready to Spray) bottles, spray cans, pints, quarts, and gallons. If purchasing Basecoat colors in RTS
(Ready to Spray) bottles, these do not require reducer- they are ready to spray. Tropical Glitz Basecoats
are heat resistant up to 400 degrees Fahrenheit. Our Basecoat colors are available in Abyss Black,
Galactic Silver, Snow White, Comet Silver, Mercury Silver, Market Rose Gold, Eclipse Blue, Cognac
Delight, Luna Blue, Yellow Submarine, Chili-Red- Hot, Titanic Blue, Mandarin, Chili Red- Medium, and
Chili-Red Mild.


## Candy Basecoats

Candy Basecoats
Tropical Glitz Candy Basecoats are manufactured using top-quality ingredients with the perfect blend of
premium pigments to achieve that vibrant candy appearance you desire. Your choice of ground coat
plays a crucial role in the final outcome. Opting for black primer/basecoat yields a deeper, richer color,
while a light grey or white primer/basecoat delivers a lighter, brighter look. For optimal results, we
recommend mixing our candy basecoats with any of our Premium Reducers at a 1:1 ratio. Make sure to
select a reducer that corresponds to your local temperature. Our Candy Basecoats are available in RTS
(Ready to Spray) bottles, spray cans, pints, quarts, and gallons. If purchasing candy colors in RTS (Ready
to Spray) bottles, these do not require reducer- they are ready to spray. If mixing the candy yourself, mix
it 1:1 with the Tropical Glitz Reducer of your choice. Remember to select the Tropical Glitz Reducer that
is suitable for the temperature where you are painting. Tropical Glitz Candy Basecoats are semi-
transparent. For best results, we recommend spraying Candy Basecoats over a black primer/basecoat.
This will yield a deeper and richer color. All Candy Basecoats are heat resistant up to 400 degrees
Fahrenheit.


## Candy and Candy Concentrates

Candy and Candy Concentrates
Tropical Glitz candy colors are manufactured using premium ingredients to achieve vibrant, transparent
colors with unmatched versatility. Your choice of ground color sets the stage for a truly custom finish.
Spraying this candy color over different ground coats creates entirely different looks. Whether you spray
it over white pearl, silver, gold, or other metallic and pearl shades, each look will be unique. While solid
ground coats work too, we highly recommend metallic and pearl ground coats for that extra dose of
sparkle. Our candy colors are available in RTS (Ready to Spray) bottles, spray cans, as well as pints,
quarts, and several colors are available as Candy Concentrates. If purchasing candy colors in RTS (Ready
to Spray) bottles, these do not require reducer- they are ready to spray. If mixing the candy yourself, mix
it 1:1 with the Tropical Glitz Reducer of your choice. Remember to select the Tropical Glitz Reducer that
is suitable for the temperature where you are painting. If mixing the Candy Concentrate, you must mix
this 8:1 (8 parts Intercoat mixed with 1 part candy concentrate, then mix that total volume 1:1 with
Reducer). For a gorgeous shimmer, spray candy colors over a metallic ground coat like, Mercury Silver,
Galactic Silver, or Comet Silver. Tropical Glitz candy colors are highly transparent with rich color clarity.
They are heat resistant up to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.


## Metal Flake Requirements Based on Project Size and Desired Coverage

Metal Flake Requirements Based on Project Size and Desired Coverage
If you are applying metal flake to a bicycle, you will need to purchase a 2.5-ounce jar to achieve 50%
coverage or a 5-ounce jar to achieve 100% coverage on the project. Additionally, you would need to mix
the metal flakes in 1 pint of intercoat clear and 1 pint of reducer. If you are applying metal flake to a
motorcycle, you will need to purchase a half pound jar to achieve 50% coverage or a one-pound jar to
achieve 100% coverage on the project. Additionally, you would need to mix the metal flakes in 1 quart of
intercoat clear and 1 quart of reducer. If you are applying metal flake to a sports bike or bagger, you will
need to purchase a 1-pound jar to achieve 50% coverage or 2 and a half pounds to achieve 100%
coverage on the project. Additionally, you would need to mix the metal flakes in 2 quarts of intercoat
clear and 2 quarts of reducer. If you are applying metal flake to a small car, you will need to purchase
one and a half pounds to achieve 50% coverage or two and a half pounds to achieve 100% coverage on
the project. Additionally, you would need to mix the metal flakes in 3 quarts of intercoat clear and 3
quarts of reducer. If you are applying metal flake to a medium size car, you will need to purchase two
and a half pounds to achieve 50% coverage or three pounds to achieve 100% coverage on the project.
Additionally, you would need to mix the metal flakes in 1 gallon of intercoat clear and 1 gallon of
reducer. If you are applying metal flake to a large car, you will need to purchase a three pounds to
achieve 50% coverage or 4 pounds to achieve 100% coverage on the project. Additionally, you would
need to mix the metal flakes in 2 gallons of intercoat clear and 2 gallons of reducer.

## Recommended Tip Sizes Based on Metal Flake Size

Recommended Tip Sizes Based on Metal Flake Size
If you are shooting .004 or .006 size metal flakes you will need a 1.3-1.4 tip size on a spray gun or a 2.0
tip size on a flake gun. If you are shooting .008 size metal flakes, you will need a 1.7 tip size on a spray
gun or a 2.0-2.5 tip size on a flake gun. If you are shooting .015 size metal flakes, you will need to use a
2.0-2.5 tip size on a flake gun. If you are shooting .025 size metal flakes, you will need to use a 2.0-2.5 tip
size on a flake gun. Large flake sizes like .040, .060, and .090 need to be shot with our El Flake Slinger Dry
Flake Gun.


## Reducers

Reducers
At Tropical Glitz we offer a variety of different reducers for you to choose from. Different reducers are
recommended depending on the ambient temperature you will be painting in. Our reducer comes in
four different varieties, each formulated for use in specific temperature ranges. Tropical Glitz Fast
Reducer is recommended for temperatures between 65°F -75°F. Medium Reducer is recommended for
temperature ranges between 75°F - 85°F. Slow Reducer is recommended for temperature ranges
between 85°F - 95°F. Extra Slow Reducer is recommended for temperatures above 95°F.
